Overview

UTV storage bags are one of the most practical upgrades you can make to a side-by-side. Factory UTVs have almost no built-in storage — a few cup holders and maybe a glove box. Once you're out on a full-day trail run, you immediately discover how much gear you actually need: tools, recovery equipment, first aid, snacks, extra layers, and more. Quality UTV-specific bags are designed to mount to roll cages, cargo beds, and rear racks without flapping, rattling, or soaking through. They also mount in positions that don't interfere with driver visibility or ingress/egress. This guide covers the best options across cage-mounted bags, cargo bed bags, and underseat solutions.

Buying Guide — What to Know Before You Buy

Mounting Method Matters

Choose a mounting method that matches your UTV's configuration. Cage bags need 1.75"–2" tube compatibility. Rear rack bags need a factory or aftermarket rack. Overhead bags need a full cage. Measure before ordering.

Waterproof Ratings Explained

Water-resistant: handles light rain. Waterproof (zippers): handles heavy rain and splashing. IPX8 waterproof: handles submersion. For UTV riders who cross streams or ride in rain, IPX8 is the minimum for electronics storage.

Capacity vs. Access Speed

Large capacity bags take longer to access. For items you need quickly — radio, tools, first aid — use a smaller cage bag or overhead bag. Reserve large rear rack bags for camping gear and extra layers.

Weight Distribution on UTVs

Heavy rear bags raise the UTV's center of gravity and can affect handling at speed. Distribute weight low and centered when possible. Avoid overloading rear rack bags on machines with high rollover risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size bags fit most UTVs?

Cage bags up to 12"x8"x6" fit most full-size UTV cages. Rear rack bags up to 24"x12"x10" work on most factory rear racks. Always measure your specific mounting points before ordering.

Are UTV bags compatible across all brands?

Most cage bags specify 1.75"–2" tube compatibility, which covers most major UTV platforms. Rear rack bags are more universal. Always verify the mounting dimensions match your specific machine.

Can I leave a UTV bag on during storage?

Yes, but remove any food, electronics, or moisture-sensitive items. Leave zippers slightly open during storage to prevent mildew. UV-resistant bag materials maintain color and integrity longer in outdoor storage.

What's the best bag for carrying tools on a UTV?

A cage-mounted bag with a tool roll insert or dedicated pockets is ideal for tools. The roll cage position keeps tools accessible without digging through a rear rack bag. KEMIMOTO and Kolpin both offer tool-specific configurations.

How do I keep a UTV bag from rattling?

Rattling usually comes from loose mounting straps or items shifting inside the bag. Use cam buckle straps rather than Velcro or bungee cords. Use internal dividers or stuff bags to prevent item movement.

Can a UTV bag hold a spare tire?

No — spare tires require a dedicated tire carrier mount, typically bolted to the rear frame or a purpose-built carrier rack. Bags can supplement tire carrier storage but can't hold a spare themselves.

Are UTV storage bags worth it vs. just using a backpack?

Bags mounted to the UTV free up your body from carrying weight — important for comfort on long rides. Backpacks also move with you when you exit the UTV, which is an advantage for day hikes. Best practice: use both for different item categories.

What material is best for UTV bags in muddy conditions?

600D polyester with PVC backing handles mud well and cleans easily with a hose. TPU-coated fabrics are more waterproof but less durable against abrasion. Cordura nylon is the most abrasion-resistant for thorny trail conditions.
